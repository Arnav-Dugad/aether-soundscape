# Field recording credits

The ambient recordings in this folder are excerpts of third-party field recordings.
Every one is either in the public domain / CC0, or licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution (CC BY). None are BY-SA, NC or ND.

**Modification note (applies to every file):** trimmed, looped, loudness-normalised and re-encoded.
Specifically: a 44–56 s excerpt was cut from the original, its tail was crossfaded into its head so it
loops seamlessly, a constant gain plus a transparent peak limiter brought it to about -20 LUFS
(quieter for the sparse fire, stream and bird recordings, so their transients are not squashed),
and it was re-encoded as 44.1 kHz MP3.
